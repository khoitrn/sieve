# Progress

Keep this short. It is read at the start of every session and is the source of truth for where things stand. Push detail into referenced files (design docs, HISTORY.jsonl).

## Current phase

One or two sentences on where the work is right now.

## Last completed

The most recent finished unit of work, with a commit reference if there is one.

## Next up

The next one to three steps, in order. This is what a session reads to know what to do without asking.

## Open questions

Decisions not yet made. Surface these to the user rather than deciding unilaterally.

## Decision log

Append one line per decision or pivot, newest last. This is how a mid-flight change of direction stays documented instead of silent. When a grill assumption is revised, record it here with the field that changed.

- <date>: <decision or pivot>.
